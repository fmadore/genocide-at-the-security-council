## Implementation integrity verdict

**Pass, with drift at the edges.** The build is still one product-specific
system: numbered plates with notes beneath, the ochre mark, Courier Prime held
to meeting symbols and paths, a type scale used without exception (no
`font-size` outside `--step-*` except relative `em` adjustments), breakpoints
held to the five DESIGN.md names (no other `@media` width in `web/src`), and no
`will-change`, no image, no drop shadow. The phone menu and the basket are built
from the same rectangle and rules as everything else.

Two DESIGN.md rules are broken in places this site's earlier audits did not
look. *The Quarantine Rule* says register hues stay inside the plates; the
Methods page uses two of them as status colours for text. *The One Warm Thing
Rule* says a magnitude ramp is paper-to-ink, never a hue; the Usage matrix fills
its cells with the accountability amber, and its comment claims, wrongly, that
`theme.ts` builds an amber ramp (it builds paper-to-ink). Both date from August.

Detector, `web/src`: `[]` (exit 0). This is still the text-only path that the
19 September correction describes, so it is weak evidence. Detector, built HTML

## Findings

### [P1] The Concordance's "to" year has no accessible name

- **Location**: `web/src/routes/concordance/+page.svelte:631-636`.
- **Category**: Accessibility. Present since 4 September; missed on 19 September.
- **Impact**: The year range is one `<label>Years <input> – <input></label>`.
  A label names only the first form control inside it, so the second box, the
  end of the range, has no name. A screen reader announces it as an unnamed
  spin button with its value; nothing says it is the end year. axe does not
  report this, because its label rule accepts any input that sits inside a
  `<label>`; that is why 95 browser tests with axe pass. It is the only label
  in `web/src` that wraps two controls.
- **WCAG**: 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value (A); 3.3.2 Labels or Instructions (A).
- **Recommendation**: A `<fieldset>` with the legend "Years" and two labels,
  "From" and "To" (visually hidden if the dash should stay), or `aria-label` on
  each box. Add an assertion on both names to the concordance test, since axe
  will not.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`.

### [P1] Three Words-in-context plates draw their text at under 5 px on a phone

- **Location**: `web/src/lib/DotPlot.svelte:31,50` (`WIDTH = 720`, fixed
  `viewBox`), and the plates "What the word is doing" (`svg.frames`) and "Which
  terms travel together" (`svg.matrix`) on `web/src/routes/language/+page.svelte`.
- **Category**: Responsive / Accessibility. Present on 19 September; the
  addendum's calendar finding was the same fault on another page.
- **Impact**: Each figure is a fixed-width SVG scaled down to the column. At
  390 px the scale is 0.48 (frames, dot plot) and 0.41 (matrix), so text set at
  10–12 SVG units renders at **4.5–4.8 CSS px**. The dot plot's 40 word labels
  are links 8 px tall stacked with no gap, and the matrix carries 56 links the
  same way. The tables beneath carry the same words and links, so nothing is
  lost outright, but the figures themselves cannot be read or tapped on a
  phone. A scan of every route at 390 px found no other SVG with text under
  9 px.
- **WCAG**: 1.4.4 Resize Text (AA) is arguable, since zoom still works; the
  site's own floor (DESIGN.md, "The Targets 24px Rule") is plainly missed.
- **Recommendation**: Do what the Chronology calendar now does: below 40rem,
  redraw at the column's width (labels at a fixed CSS size) or switch to a
  narrow layout, such as the dot plot as one row per word or the matrix as a
  list of strongest pairs.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`.

### [P2] The phone menu stays open over the page when focus leaves it

- **Location**: `web/src/routes/+layout.svelte:102-118`.
- **Category**: Accessibility. New since 19 September (the menu landed on
  19 October).
- **Impact**: At 390 px, with the menu open, Tab walks the eight sections, the
  basket and the theme toggle, then into the page, and the menu stays open,
  laid over 74–482 px of the viewport. Measured: focus landed on a link at
  480–496 px and, after Shift+Tab, on a plate title at 141–170 px, both partly
  under the open list. The menu closes on Escape (focus returns to the button,
  verified), on a page choice, and on a press outside, but not when focus
  leaves it. A keyboard user at high zoom, who gets this layout at 320 px, is
  the reader who meets it.
- **WCAG**: 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum) (AA) when the focused control
  is wholly covered; 2.4.12 (AAA) when, as measured, it is partly covered.
- **Recommendation**: Close the menu on `focusout` from the masthead when the
  new focus target is outside it, as the pointer handler already does.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`.

### [P2] The Actors and Chronology pages ship their data inside the HTML

- **Location**: `web/src/routes/actors/+page.ts` with the inlining from
  commit `ce44899` (C10, 9 October); build output `web/build/actors/index.html`.
- **Category**: Performance. New since 19 September in form; the data is the
  same.
- **Impact**: Every prerendered page now carries its load data as
  `data-sveltekit-fetched` JSON. For `/actors` that is 4.2 MB of the page's
  4.46 MB (countries 1.39 MB, speaker keyness 2.24 MB, scopes 56 KB): **586 KB
  gzipped** for the HTML alone. The other pages: Chronology 2.41 MB (377 KB
  gzipped), Usage 2.77 MB (209 KB), Words in context 1.05 MB (165 KB), the
  Overview 300 KB (67 KB). The bytes are the same ones the page used to fetch,
  so first-visit weight is roughly unchanged; what changed is that they now
  come before hydration, cannot be cached apart from the page, are sent again
  in full with every page fetch, and are parsed twice (a JSON string inside
  JSON). The speaker-keyness file covers every delegation, and the page draws
  one at a time. This pass could not compare transfer with 19 September's
  201 kB figure: the static server used here does not compress, so the gzipped
  figures above are measured on the files on disk.
- **Recommendation**: Leave the large files out of the inlining (load them in
  the browser, where the service worker can keep them), or split speaker
  keyness per delegation and fetch the one being shown. Re-run
  `web/scripts/profile-payload.mjs` on a gzip-serving preview to put a number
  on it. (The script also has a hard-coded Reader occurrence,
  `SC09822-01-009#1`, that timed out against today's build.)
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize`.

### [P2] Register hues are used outside the figures

- **Location**: `web/src/routes/methods/+page.svelte:580-586` (status text in
  `--reg-preventive` and `--reg-contentious`); `web/src/lib/UsageMatrix.svelte:234,403`
  (magnitude fill in `--reg-accountability`, with a comment at `:400-401` that
  says this is "the ramp `theme.ts` builds", which it is not: `theme.ts:355` is
  `mix(paper, ink, t)`).
- **Category**: Implementation integrity. Both date from August; missed on
  19 September, which reported the quarantine as holding.
- **Impact**: On Methods, "verified" and "open" are coloured as the preventive
  and contentious registers, so a status reads as an analytical category. It is
  also the cause of the known contrast failure (ROADMAP open item 6: 4.36:1 on
  the zebra row, re-measured today at the same value). The state tokens
  (`--state-ok`, `--state-bad`) exist for this; `--state-ok` (#4a6b2e) on the
  zebra row is about 5.5:1. On Usage, an amber magnitude ramp makes a second
  warm thing on the page, against the One Warm Thing Rule; its cells are
  amber at 1–32% opacity (measured in the browser).
- **Recommendation**: Methods: the state tokens, which also settles ROADMAP 6's
  light-mode half. Usage: the paper-to-ink ramp, or, if amber is the decision,
  write it into DESIGN.md as an exception and fix the comment. Both are
  design calls, which is why they are reported and not changed.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`.

### [P3] A skipped heading level on Actors

- **Location**: `web/src/lib/SpeakerKeyness.svelte:289`.
- **Category**: Accessibility.
- **Impact**: "Most frequent agenda categories" is an `h4` directly under the
  delegation's `h2`; the component's next heading, "Who is not here", is an
  `h3`. A screen-reader user moving by heading sees a level missing.
- **Recommendation**: Make it an `h3`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`.

### [P3] The Concordance's year strip has 3 px buttons on a phone

- **Location**: `.strip` in `web/src/routes/concordance/+page.svelte`.
- **Category**: Responsive.
- **Impact**: At 390 px the 79 year bars are buttons 3.2 × 56 px, touching. A
  tap picks a neighbouring year as often as the intended one. They pass WCAG
  2.5.8 under its "equivalent control" exception, because the year boxes do
  the same job; that exception is weakened while the second box has no name
  (P1 above). Keyboard use is a single roving stop, which is right.
- **Recommendation**: Below 40rem, treat the strip as a picture and leave the
  choosing to the year boxes, or group bars by decade.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`.

### [P3] One browser test is flaky on a cold dev server

- **Location**: `web/e2e/tests/browsing.spec.ts:104-128`, "ranking pages,
  search and full CSV retain every speaker".
- **Category**: Implementation integrity (test harness).
- **Impact**: In the full run it timed out after 30 s: the Next button stayed
  disabled because the table held the real 20 rows, not the 25-row
  substitute. It passed twice on re-run. This looks like the race that commit
  `ce44899` describes for a Usage test: a click on "Actors" before the router
  has started becomes a full page load answered by the server, which the
  route interception never sees. CI fails on flaky tests
  (`failOnFlakyTests`), so this will cost a run.
- **Recommendation**: Wait for hydration before the click, as the Usage test
  now does.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`.

### [P3] Small departures from the named scales

- **Location**: Spacing literals off the `--sp-*` scale:
  `routes/reader/[meeting]/+page.svelte:904,912,929,978` (`0.05rem 0.4rem`,
  `2.9rem`), `lib/DiffusionChart.svelte:155,238` (`2.4rem`, `1.4rem`),
  `routes/+layout.svelte:303` (`0.3rem`), `routes/semantic/+page.svelte:444,476`
  (`0.4rem`, and `0.5rem` written out rather than `--sp-2`),
  `routes/chronology/+page.svelte:718` (a tooltip `<hr>` with inline `4px` and
  `opacity:.2`). A 2 px rule (`lib/DiffusionChart.svelte:142`,
  `lib/Figure.svelte:580` in print) where DESIGN.md allows 1 px and 3 px only.
  Round legend swatches (`border-radius: 50%`, 0.65rem) on the Semantic map,
  where every other key is a 0.625rem square; they do match the map's round
  dots, so this may be intended. Two micro-transitions with no reduced-motion
  guard: the Reader's speech chevron (`:921`) and the concordance button's icon
  nudge (`:1397`); DESIGN.md says the disclosure chevron's turn is switched off
  under reduced motion. The Actors disclosure "Why 10 ranked speakers are not
  mapped" is 21 px tall, under the site's 2rem control floor (it passes WCAG
  2.5.8 on spacing).
- **Category**: Implementation integrity.
- **Impact**: None for a reader. They are the kind of drift that turns a scale
  into a suggestion.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`.

## Patterns and systemic issues

- **Fixed-width SVG drawn in markup.** The ECharts figures size their text in
  CSS pixels; the hand-drawn SVGs scale theirs with the `viewBox`. The calendar
  was fixed one at a time; the dot plot, frames and matrix on Words in context
  are the same fault. A shared rule (draw at the measured width, or switch
  layout below 40rem) would stop the next one.
- **axe is not the whole accessibility test.** It missed the unnamed input
  because an input inside a `<label>` passes its rule. The concordance's form
  needs an explicit assertion on names.
- **Register hues leak where nobody looks.** The figures are clean; the leaks
  are in a status table and a matrix ramp, both outside what a plate review
  covers.
